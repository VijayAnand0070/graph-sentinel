## Research and hardening programme (2026-09-17)

Six phases, run against the real 543,615-event corpus with every number carrying
an interval and every claim pinned by a test. Findings are numbered in
`DETECTION_RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md`.

| Phase | State | Evidence | Remaining gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 — identity vs behaviour | Ablation complete; training variants running | Score-time ablation over every memory channel and the dominant features: zeroing 8426's identity channels leaves its recall at 82–95%. The model learned one campaign's behaviour, not a host ID (Finding 20; Finding 14's mechanism corrected). `use_memory` and `exclude_train_src_hosts` controls added to the pipeline and proven by test. | Memoryless, held-out-host and control training runs finish (~2 h each on the RTX 3050); their caches are rescored and compared paired |
| 2 — prevention instrument | Complete; measured | `simulation/`: benign traffic resampled from measured behaviour, attacker hosts with benign history, campaigns with ground truth in hops, the production composition as scorer. 100 campaigns: 20–21% detected, median first alert at hop 4, 0.75% of hops prevented; every detected chain was the rule, not the model; detection collapses beyond 120 s between hops (Finding 21) | Policy pricing for a rule floor at the gate; retrain with a second campaign pattern (open item 13) |
| 3 — technique validation | Instrument complete; full run in progress | ATT&CK-derived scenarios for T1110 (guessing and spraying), T1087, T1078, T1550.002, T1550.003, T1078.002, built from the behaviour and not the rule; per-technique detection recall, attribution recall, benign false-attribution rate and precision (Finding 22) | Fix or retire signatures that do not fire on their own technique |
| 4 — the execution gate | Complete | `detection/gates.py`: derived on validation at a 75% Wilson lower bound on the fused risk the product compares (0.846394; first derived on the raw probability as 0.877729, corrected by the end-to-end check), realised test precision 53.2% [46.3%, 60.0%] and false-positive rate recorded beside it, re-derived by test. | 6 — temporal robustness and importance | Complete via Phases 1 and 4 | Per-day decay measured (PR-AUC 0.98 → 0.64 over four days, same attacker); feature dependence measured by ablation rather than permutation | A retraining-cadence experiment once a second attack pattern exists |
